§ Legal notice

This app records conversations (microphone + system audio), transcribes them, and sends audio, transcripts and, if used, screenshots to external AI services (Replicate) for processing.

Using this app is only permitted if ALL conversation participants have given their explicit prior consent to the recording and processing.

Secretly recording non-public spoken words is a criminal offence in many jurisdictions (e.g. § 201 of the German Criminal Code — violation of the confidentiality of the spoken word); data-protection laws such as the GDPR apply additionally. This app is intended for practice and mock interviews. You bear full responsibility for using it lawfully.
